MAVIC 4 PRO

100 megapixels. Three lenses. Zero compromises.

Week 1 was about drones under 250g. This is the other end of the scale: a 1,063g flagship that carries a 100MP Hasselblad camera, two telephoto lenses, and a gimbal that rotates a full 360°. It does not try to be small. It tries to be the best camera DJI has ever put in the air — and it flies for 51 minutes while doing it. From £1,879.

The cameras

Three cameras. One flight. No landing to change lenses.

A beginner page lists specs; a flagship page paces them. Each camera gets its own beat — its own crop, its own job.

28mm

Hasselblad — the wide shot.

100MP · 4/3-inch CMOS · f/2.0–f/11 adjustable aperture

6K/60fps HDR with 15.5 stops of dynamic range, finished with the Hasselblad Natural Colour Solution — colour science from the medium-format world, not a phone filter.

The landscape, real-estate and print camera — the one that makes the other two look like bonuses.

70mm

Medium tele — the portrait lens.

48MP · 1/1.3-inch CMOS · f/2.8

Compression without getting close: faces, details, and subjects you cannot (or should not) fly right up to.

168mm

Tele — the reach.

50MP · 1/1.5-inch CMOS · f/2.8

Wildlife, architecture details, and shots from a distance that stays legal and safe. 4K/100fps on the tele, 4K/120fps on the wide and medium tele.

All three cameras shoot 10-bit D-Log / D-Log M / HLG, with Dual Native ISO Fusion and up to 5-frame RAW stacking on the main camera.

Infinity Gimbal

A gimbal that rolls all the way round.

The ball-shaped Infinity Gimbal rotates a full 360°, so the camera can roll through a shot instead of just panning — plus up to 70° of upward tilt for building facades and bridges, and true vertical video without cropping the sensor. One rolling shot, one upward tilt, one vertical frame — imagine the footage.

  • 3-axis stabilisation
  • Subject focusing, all three lenses
  • Free Panoramas
  • Up to 70° upward tilt

Flight & safety

51 minutes. 30 kilometres. And it sees in the dark.

Up to 51 minutes of flight (45 minutes hovering) means a whole location scout, a time-lapse, and a panorama on one battery — fewer battery swaps, fewer missed moments.

DJI O4+ carries a 10-bit HDR feed out to 30 km under FCC rules (15 km CE/SRRC/MIC — check your region before quoting range).

Safety is omnidirectional vision sensing down to 0.1 lux (Nightscape), with forward LiDAR and downward infrared on top — but at 1,063g this is not a sub-250g drone: registration and operator rules apply (see FAQ).

Flight times are lab ratings in calm conditions; real-world mixed flying is shorter — reviewers report roughly 40–45 minutes in practice. Range figures are ideal-condition FCC/CE figures, not a promise in a built-up area.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this really that much better than the Mini 5 Pro from Day 1?+

Yes, in camera terms — and the page should not hide the trade-off. The Mini 5 Pro is 249.9g with a 1-inch sensor; the Mavic 4 Pro is 1,063g with a 100MP 4/3-inch Hasselblad plus two tele lenses. You are trading pocketability and light regulation for image quality, reach, and flight time. That is the whole Week 2 step-up.

Do I need to register it?+

Yes — at 1,063g it is well over the 250g threshold, so it needs a Flyer ID and an Operator ID in the UK/EU system and registration under FAA rules in the US. Check your local rules before you fly; in Nigeria, check NCAA requirements.

Can I buy it in the US?+

Unsettled — sources conflict. Some US retailers list it (around $2,199–$2,899 depending on kit) while other coverage says DJI did not officially sell it in the US at launch. Check a retailer that ships to you before quoting one. The prices on this page are UK DJI Store prices.

How long does it really fly?+

Rated up to 51 minutes in lab conditions (45 minutes hovering). Real-world mixed flying is less — plan shoots around roughly 40–45 minutes per battery.

What does the Creator Combo's 512GB actually buy?+

High-speed onboard storage for ALL-I 4:2:2 video and time-lapse at 1-second intervals, plus the RC Pro 2's brighter 7-inch screen. The standard and Fly More kits have 64GB built in and still take a microSD card.

Flagship money. Flagship images. No compromises left to explain.
